# Page checklist (before you publish)

- [ ] Answers one clear question, phrased the way customers ask it
- [ ] Direct answer in the first one or two sentences under the main heading
- [ ] Every section makes sense on its own and names the subject (no orphan "it")
- [ ] Specific facts: numbers with units, prices with currency and date, named integrations
- [ ] Comparable facts in a table
- [ ] Sources cited for claims; quotes attributed to named, credentialed people
- [ ] Limitations or "who it's not for" stated where relevant
- [ ] Visible "Last updated" date, and the page actually reviewed on that date
- [ ] Content present in raw HTML (no JS-only text); images have descriptive alt text; video has a transcript
- [ ] Canonical URL set; in the sitemap; internally linked from related pages
- [ ] Structured data, if any, matches the visible content exactly
- [ ] Nothing hidden from users that is shown to crawlers (and the reverse)
