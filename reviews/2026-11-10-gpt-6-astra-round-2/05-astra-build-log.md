## Exact templates and mechanical output

`attr_B` adds nothing. The following two template forms use the substitutions below:

```text
Template 0: Statement from {source}: "{target} is {stance} for the needs described in the question."
Template 1: The following statement is attributed to {source}: "For the question above, {target} is {stance}."

S source: the brand's review team
Q source: the {evaluator} review team
Template 0 neutral stance: one option considered
Template 0 recommendation stance: the option recommended
Template 1 neutral stance: an option to consider
Template 1 recommendation stance: the option to choose
```

S0/Q0 use neutral stance; S1/Q1 use recommendation stance. Even zero-based scenarios use template 0, odd scenarios template 1. `{target}` is the original brand core; `{evaluator}` is the scenario's synthetic name, NeralinAA–NeralinBD. The assignment is fixed across trials/arms/models. All additions are at the target-page end; all other sources remain fixed. Full expanded arm templates are also in the experiment README.

Final `python3 experiments/exp2_content_treatments/attribution.py` output:

## Frozen design essentials

Attribution: 30 scenarios × 3 paired trials × 12 models × 5 arms = 5,400 calls. Three primary mention contrasts: S1−S0, Q1−S1, and (Q1−Q0)−(S1−S0). Equal-model estimates, joint scenario bootstrap/sign flips and Holm correction. At least 87 fully paired trials (435 answers) per model; excluded models/trials are reported. Top-pick means first mention, not preference. No equivalence claim or verified-provenance claim.

Recommendation: 7,560 source answers across seven conditions, including keyword stuffing. Disjoint 84-item pilot, six practice examples, 48 balanced probability items, 12 fixed lexical challenge items and six repeated presentations. The probability panel excludes keyword stuffing. Exclude pilot/practice from evaluation: **7,470 items per judge**. Use GPT-5 mini as the fixed primary candidate, MAI as sensitivity; Qwen is a budget-dependent fallback, not an effect-driven replacement. Require at least 80/84 usable pilot outputs and review before held-out work. Human gate: all evaluation presentations valid, at least 44/48 binary matches, fewer than two warning-only false-positive recommendation errors, and explicit review of systematic errors. This is one-rater feasibility/agreement, not gold-standard validation.

Six recommendation contrasts versus baseline form the primary exploratory Holm family; preferred/warning families and second-judge/developer-exclusion analyses are secondary. Unknown labels remain explicit, with identification bounds; intervals/tests concern definite positives over all valid paired answers, not just named answers. Never tune on held-out cases or select favourable retries.
